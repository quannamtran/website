About the book

When algorithms join the conversation

From the boardroom to the hospital, from our playlists to our investments, data and artificial intelligence now play a part in the choices we make every day. They promise speed, precision and objectivity. They also bring new blind spots: biased data, opaque models, and the temptation to hand over our judgment altogether.

Augmenting Decision explores what happens when people and algorithms decide together. It shows how to combine what machines do best with what only humans can do, so that technology sharpens our judgment instead of replacing it.

About the author

Antoine Tran Quan Nam

I’m Antoine Tran Quan Nam, and much of my career has been driven by one question: how do we turn intelligence into impact?

I started as a data scientist, working with engineering, statistics, and models. Later, I moved into AI product management and digital transformation, leading initiatives from an initial idea through to scaled implementation.

That journey taught me that the technology is rarely the hardest part.

A powerful model means little if we are solving the wrong problem, using data that poorly represents reality, optimizing for the wrong outcome, or placing AI into a workflow that was never designed to use it. The real challenge is connecting analytical intelligence with human judgment, action, and accountability.

Music has taught me something similar. I’m also an amateur pianist, have recorded an album with my band, and work with musicians exploring how AI can become part of the creative process. AI can generate ideas, variations, and possibilities. But it cannot tell us which idea feels authentic or which one is worth pursuing.

That decision remains human.

This intersection between data and judgment, AI and responsibility, technology and creativity is what led me to write Augmenting Decision.

The book is ultimately built around a simple belief:

The future of decision-making is not about AI deciding for us. It is about humans learning to decide better with AI.
