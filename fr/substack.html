Notes

Réflexions courtes et travaux en cours, entre deux publications.

Antoine Tran Quan Nam

@antoinetranquannam · 22 novembre 2026

The real disruption isn't a chatbot that writes emails faster, it's the possibility that a decade of biomedical research gets compressed into eighteen months. Organizations still budgeting for marginal productivity gains are planning for the wrong kind of change. The harder question isn't whether models get smarter, it's whether institutions, regulators, and labor markets can absorb progress that no longer arrives on a human research timeline. Abundance delivered too fast looks a lot like disruption.
